# Getting started

## 1. Add your music

**On the PC:** On first start click **Choose music folder** and pick the folder with your music
(or **Use default music folder**). Amber Player reads all subfolders. Add more folders under
**Settings → Music folders**.

**On the phone:** After you grant access, the app automatically reads the **Music** folder
(`/storage/emulated/0/Music`).

Scanning runs in the background – you can start right away. New, changed and deleted files are
picked up at every start. To scan manually: **Settings → Rescan**.

> **Good to know:** Files with the same name are **not** merged. Two songs that happen to share a
> name stay two songs.

File names from online converters like `yt5s.com - Artist - Title (128 kbps).mp3` are cleaned
up to “Artist – Title” automatically. If the artist tag is missing, Amber Player splits
“Artist - Title” names.

## 2. Play

- **On the PC** double-click a track, **on the phone** tap it.
- **Play** plays the whole view, **Shuffle** in random order.
- The player shows cover, title, progress, volume and **Up next**.
- **On the phone** tapping the mini player opens the large “Now playing” view.

## 3. Browse

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Tracks** | all songs, sortable by artist, title, album or recently added |
| **Albums**, **Artists**, **Genres** | grouped by the tags in your files |
| **Favourites** | all tracks with a heart |
| **History** | recently played and most played |
| **Playlists** | your own lists |
| **Radio** | internet radio, see [Internet radio](Internet-Radio.md) |

On the phone, artists, genres, favourites, history, effects and settings are under **More**.

## 4. Playlists

1. **Playlists → New playlist**, enter a name.
2. Add tracks:
   - **On the PC:** drag tracks onto the playlist or right-click → **Add to playlist**. While
     dragging, a drop zone with all playlists appears at the bottom.
   - **On the phone:** long-press a track → **Add to playlist**.
3. Inside a playlist, reorder tracks by dragging (PC) and remove them.

Playlists can be **exported** and **imported** as M3U – to exchange them with other players.

## 5. Sound and look

- **Effects** (sliders icon at the top): equalizer, bass booster and more – see [Sound effects](Sound-Effects.md).
- **Visualizer** (key V or the icon in the player), see [Visualizers](Visualizers.md).
- **Settings → Appearance:** language, accent colour, dark/light, full screen on the phone.
